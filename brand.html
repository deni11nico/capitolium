Brand exploration

Five names, five marks

Candidates to replace “Capitolium” — each rooted in real estate and architecture, drawn in the site’s existing forest-green and matte-brass palette, and shown on both dark and light grounds. Every logo is pure SVG, ready to drop into the header.

No. 01

Aedis

Latin for “edifice, dwelling”

Keeps the classical gravity of “Capitolium” but shorter, easier to say, and directly about a building rather than a hill in Rome. The mark is a distilled pediment-and-columns façade — a nod to the property’s heritage architecture.

No. 02

Atriu

The courtyard at the heart of the house

Romanian spelling of atrium — the open inner court of a Roman house, and literally what this property has. The mark is the plan view: nested frames around a brass center, six walls sheltering one shared space.

No. 03

Fronton

The crown of a façade

The architectural term (identical in Romanian and French) for the decorative gable above a grand entrance. It sounds established and speaks the language of restoration. The mark repeats the gable line, rising like rooflines in Oradea’s old center.

No. 04

Curtea

“The Court” — Romanian, warm, rooted

The only fully Romanian candidate. Curtea means both the courtyard and, historically, a noble residence — Curtea Domnească. Instantly legible to local buyers, quietly aristocratic. The mark is the arched carriage gateway with its brass keystone: the moment you step off the street into the property.

No. 05

Domenia

From domus & domain

An invented name that reads naturally in Romanian and English — built from domus (house) and domeniu (estate, domain). Feels like a modern property brand that could outgrow a single building. The mark is a tall paneled entrance door with a single brass handle: ownership, arrival, home.

Each mark is drawn as inline SVG — dark and light variants — in the site’s existing forest & brass palette.
